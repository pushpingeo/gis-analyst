# Requests to the atlas

Tobler and the atlas are worked on in separate chats (decided 17 Nov). Tobler doesn't edit the atlas repo.
What Tobler can rely on is written down in the atlas repo's `docs/mcp-tools.md`. When Tobler needs something
new from the atlas, add it here (newest first), tell Rob, and he passes it to the atlas chat.

- **17 Nov, parked by Rob: a name index.** A nightly harvest of feature names and outlines from named-feature
  layers (licence blocks, fields, protected areas, wind leases) into the atlas database, so a named search is one
  query instead of asking up to 40 live services. Needs a licence check first: it copies names and outlines rather
  than pointing at the services.
